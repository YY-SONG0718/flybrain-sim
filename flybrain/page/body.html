What you're looking at

Each dot is one neuron at its real position in the FAFB brain volume, from the FlyWire annotation release. The bright cluster sits on the ventral midline — the sub-esophageal zone, which is where fly taste processing actually lives. Nothing places it there but the data. {{READOUT}} is the one orange dot: it lights only when {{READOUT}} itself fires. Blue glow near the tick is its neighbours — about a hundred neurons sit within 13 µm of it at this scale.

Why the body is stylised

The proboscis is a kinematic rig, not physics: MN9's firing rate, low-pass filtered, sets the joint angles. A real embodied version needs MuJoCo — NeuroMechFly or flybody — which is the obvious next step and the thing nobody has yet closed the loop on.

What the model doesn't know

Synapse count stands in for synaptic weight. There is no neuromodulation, no plasticity, no intrinsic cell diversity, and neurotransmitter signs are predictions that are right about 87% of the time. Trust which neurons respond and the sign of an effect; not the absolute rates.
